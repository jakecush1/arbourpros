About

Est. 2020

Meet Bergen & Emma

ArbourPros Trees is a small, local tree care business run by two ISA Certified Arborists.

We started ArbourPros in 2020 because we love working with trees and wanted to offer homeowners professional, certified tree care at a price that makes sense.

When you hire us, you get the people you talked to on the phone doing the work. We take time to explain what your trees need, give you options, and leave your yard clean when we’re done.

How we work

Our approach

Safety first

We plan every job around keeping people, homes and property safe, and we use proper climbing and rigging gear on every climb.

Tree health

As certified arborists we follow industry pruning standards, so your trees stay strong and healthy for years after we leave.

Fair pricing

No surprises. You get a clear estimate before any work starts, with affordable rates for homeowners.
