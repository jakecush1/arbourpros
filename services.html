Services

Professional tree care from ISA Certified Arborists, at affordable, low-cost rates.

Tree removal

Safe removal of dead, dying, hazardous or unwanted trees, including careful piece-by-piece takedowns close to houses, garages and fences.

Pruning & trimming

Deadwood removal, crown cleaning, thinning and clearance pruning for roofs, driveways and yards, done to industry standards.

Climbing & tight-access work

Backyard trees and spots a bucket truck can’t reach. We climb the tree and lower pieces down safely.

Hazard tree assessment

Worried about a leaning, cracked or dying tree? We’ll take a look and give you honest advice on whether it should stay or go.

Storm damage cleanup

Broken limbs and fallen trees cleared quickly and safely after wind and snow storms.

Brush & debris cleanup

We clean up after ourselves, and can haul away branches and brush from your property.

Have elm trees? Saskatchewan bans elm pruning from April 1 to August 31 each year to slow the spread of Dutch elm disease. Plan elm pruning for fall or winter, and never keep or move elm firewood.
